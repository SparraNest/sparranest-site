Questions

Good to know

Is the audit really free?

Yes. Sign up with your name and email and you'll get the app straight away. You'll also be first to hear when the Hidden Value System opens.

Do I need Excel or any experience?

No. Everything runs in the app on your phone, with plain-English explanations of terms like ARV and ROI. An Excel version is included with the System for anyone who prefers a laptop.

Who can see the homes I save?

No one but you. Your homes, scores, notes and photos are saved only on your own phone. They're never sent to me or to your agent. Read the privacy page.

I got a code from my agent. What do I do?

Tap the link your agent sent you, add your email, and the System unlocks. You can also open the app and enter your code under System.

Is this financial advice?

No. The audit and System are educational tools to help you think clearly. Always confirm numbers with your inspector, contractor, lender and agent before you buy.
